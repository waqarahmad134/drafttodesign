Whether you are remodeling a kitchen or building from scratch, a dimensioned floor plan is usually the first drawing you need. Here is what floor plan drafting costs in 2026 and what affects the price.

## Typical floor plan prices in 2026

- **Simple as-built plan (existing home):** $99 to $300. One floor, straightforward layout, drawn from your measurements or sketches.
- **Remodel or addition plan:** $200 to $600. Includes the proposed changes, demolitions and new construction notes.
- **New construction plan set:** $500 to $2,000+. Multiple floors, elevations, roof plan and sections, depending on home size.
- **Rush delivery:** add 25 to 50 percent for 24 to 48 hour turnaround.

## What affects the price

1. **Home size and complexity.** A 1,200 sq ft ranch is faster to draw than a 4,000 sq ft two-story with angles and curves.
2. **Source material quality.** Accurate measurements or existing drawings mean less guesswork. Rough sketches take longer to interpret.
3. **Level of detail.** Basic wall layouts are quick. Full furniture layouts, electrical plans and finish notes add hours.
4. **Revisions.** Most drafters include one or two revision rounds. Know what is included before you order.

## As-built vs proposed plans

An **as-built plan** documents what exists right now. A **proposed plan** shows what will change. Remodels need both: the as-built as a starting point, and the proposed design for contractors and permits. Ordering them together is cheaper than ordering them separately.

## Do you need a licensed architect for a floor plan?

For most residential remodels and additions, a skilled drafter is enough to produce the drawings. Structural changes, new construction and anything requiring a permit stamp are different: those need a licensed architect or engineer, depending on your state and local rules. A good drafting service will tell you honestly when your project crosses that line.

## How to keep costs down

- Measure carefully and send clear sketches with all dimensions
- Decide on the layout before ordering, not during revisions
- Bundle the floor plan with 3D views if you will need them later
- Respond to review drafts quickly so the project does not stall

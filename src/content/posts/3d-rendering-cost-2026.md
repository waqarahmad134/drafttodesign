If you are planning a build or remodel, you have probably wondered what a photorealistic 3D render actually costs. Prices vary widely, so here is an honest breakdown of 2026 market rates and what affects them.

## Typical 3D rendering prices in 2026

- **Single exterior render:** $150 to $600 per view. A straightforward daytime view of a house exterior sits at the low end. Dusk or twilight views with detailed landscaping cost more.
- **Single interior render:** $150 to $500 per view. Kitchens and living rooms with custom materials take longer than simple bedrooms.
- **Full 3D model (no renders):** $200 to $800 depending on size and detail. This is the raw model you can orbit and view from any angle.
- **Complete package (model + 5 to 10 renders):** $500 to $2,000 for a typical single-family home.

## What drives the price up

1. **Level of detail.** Custom furniture, realistic materials and fine trim work all add modeling hours.
2. **Number of views.** Each camera angle is a separate render that needs lighting and post-processing.
3. **Revisions.** Most studios include one or two revision rounds. Extra rounds add cost.
4. **Turnaround speed.** Rush delivery (48 hours) usually carries a premium of 25 to 50 percent.
5. **Source quality.** Clean architectural drawings are faster to work from than rough sketches or verbal descriptions.

## What drives the price down

- Providing accurate floor plans and clear reference photos
- Approving the 3D model before rendering starts (avoids re-renders)
- Bundling multiple views of the same project instead of ordering one at a time

## Interior vs exterior: which costs more?

Roughly the same per view. Interiors can actually cost more because of the sheer number of objects: furniture, fixtures, decor and materials all need to be modeled or sourced. A bare exterior with simple landscaping is faster.

## How to get an accurate quote

Send the rendering studio your floor plans (or even hand sketches), a few reference images of the style you want, and tell them how many views you need and when you need them. With that information, a good studio quotes within 24 hours.

## What you get

A precise 3D model of your space or building, built to real dimensions from your floor plans, sketches or reference photos. Walls, openings, roofs, stairs, cabinetry and key fixtures are all modeled, ready for rendering or design development.

## Perfect for

- Seeing your project in three dimensions before committing to finishes
- The base every photorealistic render is built on
- Builders showing clients exactly what will be built
- Product and furniture visualization

## How it works

1. You send plans, sketches or measurements
2. We build the 3D model to scale
3. You review screenshots from multiple angles
4. We refine, then hand over the model

## Deliverables

- 3D model in SketchUp (SKP) and/or FBX/OBJ
- Preview screenshots from key angles
- 2 revision rounds included
